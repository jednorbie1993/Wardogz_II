WARDOGZ II
DOCUMENT 0081 - ENEMY WAVE PROGRESSION
=======================================

PURPOSE
-------
This document records the planned Stage 1 enemy-wave structure for Wardogz II.

GENERAL WAVE DESIGN
-------------------
- Stage progress is used to trigger enemy waves.
- The first discussed trigger points include:
  * 9% stage progress
  * 18% stage progress
  * 27% stage progress
- Later waves continue as the player advances toward the Vargas section.
- Vargas' intro sequence begins around 86% stage progress.
- Vargas' actual active position is later in the stage, around world X = 11452
  (approximately 98% of the stage in the current test implementation).

ENEMY TYPES USED
----------------
- Punk
- Hooligan
- Gangster

EARLY WAVE PLAN
---------------
9%:
- 1 Hooligan

18%:
- 2 Hooligans
- 1 additional reinforcement is planned after the initial enemies enter.

27%:
- This is the next mixed-enemy wave.
- Punk begins to be included in the progression.
- Exact final composition can be tuned in code without changing the wave system.

PACING RULE
-----------
- Do not dump every enemy on-screen at the same instant.
- Use an initial group plus delayed reinforcements.
- Keep the fight readable and arcade-like rather than overcrowded.
- The working target discussed is roughly 3-4 active normal enemies at a time.
- New enemies should enter naturally after room becomes available.

TECHNICAL SLOT PLAN
-------------------
Current wave-test structure provides six enemy slots:
- Slots 0-4: normal Stage 1 enemies and reinforcements
- Slot 5: reserved for Vargas

DESIGN GOAL
-----------
The player should feel that enemy pressure increases as the stage advances:
1. Simple opening encounter
2. Larger same-type encounter
3. Mixed enemy waves
4. Reinforcement pressure
5. Stronger pre-boss encounters
6. Vargas transition

NOTES
-----
- Enemy composition and percentages should remain easy to tune manually.
- Wave triggers should be based on world/stage progress, not the visible progress-map UI.
- The camera system and enemy-wave system should remain independent.
