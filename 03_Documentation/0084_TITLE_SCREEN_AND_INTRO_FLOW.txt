==================================================
2. TITLE SCREEN
==================================================

Main elements:
- WARDOGZ II logo
- Jamber in a 3/4 side-view pose
- Jamber is screaming / powering up
- Berserker awakening feeling
- Blue energy aura
- Red rage accents
- Dark alley / smoky street background
- PRESS ENTER TO CONTINUE

Composition direction:
- Jamber should NOT be literally centered.
- Jamber should occupy one side of the screen, preferably the left or left-center area.
- The WARDOGZ II logo should have enough clear space to remain readable.
- PRESS ENTER TO CONTINUE should be placed in the lower area and remain easy to see.

Visual identity:
- The title image should feel like a fighting-game power-up moment.
- Blue represents stored energy / Berserker meter.
- Red represents rage, aggression, and the damage-boost state.
- The image also acts as visual foreshadowing for Jamber's future Berserker mechanic.

==================================================
3. INPUT FLOW
==================================================

Game launch
    -> Title screen appears
    -> Player assets needed for the title screen are ready
    -> Wait for ENTER
    -> ENTER pressed
    -> Story sequence begins
    -> Story pages advance
    -> Remaining Stage 1 assets can load during the story
    -> Tutorial screen appears
    -> Player confirms / continues
    -> Stage 1 begins

==================================================
4. DESIGN RULE
==================================================

The opening must feel cinematic but should not delay the player too much.
The 7-story-image format is the current target because it provides enough story while keeping the intro short.
