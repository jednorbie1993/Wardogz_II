WARDOGZ II
DOCUMENT 0083 - VARGAS PRE-BOSS WAVE FLOW
==========================================

PURPOSE
-------
This document records the transition from the final normal Stage 1 enemy waves
into the Vargas boss section.

VARGAS RESERVATION
------------------
Current enemy-slot plan:
- Slots 0-4 = normal enemies / reinforcements
- Slot 5 = Vargas

Vargas should not consume a normal reinforcement slot.

PRE-BOSS SECTION
----------------
As the player approaches the late part of Stage 1:
- Normal enemy pressure should increase.
- Mixed Punk / Hooligan / Gangster encounters can be used.
- Reinforcements should still enter from off-screen.
- Do not overcrowd the arena immediately before the boss.

86% TRIGGER
-----------
Around 86% stage progress:
- Begin the Vargas transition / intro section.
- Stop starting unnecessary new normal waves.
- Allow remaining active normal enemies to be resolved as required by the final
  boss-transition logic.
- Prepare the camera/scene for Vargas.

VARGAS ACTIVE POSITION
----------------------
Current test implementation places Vargas activation later in the stage:
- world X approximately 11452
- approximately 98% of the current stage length

This means:
- 86% = boss-intro / pre-boss transition point
- later position near 98% = Vargas becomes the active encounter

INTENDED FLOW
-------------
1. Player clears the late normal wave.
2. Player advances toward approximately 86%.
3. Vargas intro/transition begins.
4. Normal reinforcement spawning is disabled for the boss section.
5. Player proceeds into the boss area.
6. Vargas activates.
7. Boss battle begins.

SPAWN SAFETY
------------
Once the Vargas transition has started:
- Do not spawn a new Punk/Hooligan/Gangster wave behind the player unless it is
  intentionally part of the boss design.
- Vargas remains reserved in his dedicated slot.
- Normal enemies should not accidentally overwrite or initialize Vargas' slot.

LOADING
-------
Vargas assets should be ready before the player reaches the boss activation area.

The story/loading/title system may later be used to preload general Stage 1
assets, but the gameplay must still guarantee that Vargas is loaded before his
intro/activation.

DESIGN GOAL
-----------
The end of Stage 1 should feel deliberate:

normal street fight
    -> stronger late wave
    -> short breathing/transition space
    -> Vargas introduction
    -> boss battle

This prevents the boss from feeling like just another enemy spawned inside a
normal wave.
