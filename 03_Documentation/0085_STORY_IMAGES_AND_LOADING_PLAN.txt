WARDOGZ II - DOCUMENT 0074
STORY IMAGES AND LOADING PLAN
Date: 2026-08-15

PURPOSE
This document defines the 7 story images shown after the title screen and how they can also serve as a loading period for Stage 1 assets.

==================================================
1. STORY IMAGE 1 - THE CITY AT NIGHT
==================================================

Scene:
- Dark city street / alley at night
- Smoke, streetlights, damaged or neglected surroundings
- Quiet but dangerous atmosphere

Purpose:
- Establish the world and mood before the enemies appear.

==================================================
2. STORY IMAGE 2 - TROUBLE IN THE STREETS
==================================================

Scene:
- Punk, Gangster, and Hooligan presence in the streets
- They should look like the local threat controlling the area
- Civilians may be absent, distant, or intimidated

Purpose:
- Introduce the Stage 1 enemy threat.

==================================================
3. STORY IMAGE 3 - JAMBER ARRIVES
==================================================

Scene:
- Jamber enters the area
- Serious / focused expression
- Medium or wide cinematic shot

Purpose:
- Give Jamber a clear hero entrance.

==================================================
4. STORY IMAGE 4 - NO MORE RUNNING
==================================================

Scene:
- Closer shot of Jamber
- Determined expression
- He has made the decision to fight

Purpose:
- Show Jamber's resolve before combat.

==================================================
5. STORY IMAGE 5 - THE FIRST WAVE
==================================================

Scene:
- Jamber faces the first group of enemies
- Punk, Gangster, and Hooligan are ready to fight
- Strong confrontation composition

Purpose:
- Transition the story toward direct combat.

==================================================
6. STORY IMAGE 6 - BERSERKER POWER AWAKENS
==================================================

Scene:
- Jamber begins releasing stored energy
- Blue aura builds around him
- Red rage accents begin appearing
- His expression becomes more aggressive

Purpose:
- Foreshadow the Berserker system that will later become a gameplay mechanic.

Current Berserker concept:
- Jamber has HP plus a separate blue energy meter.
- The energy meter increases through combat activity such as dealing damage and/or receiving damage.
- When the meter becomes full, Berserker Mode can activate.
- Berserker Mode gives Jamber increased damage.
- Jamber may gain a slightly red-tinted rage appearance during the mode.

NOTE:
The exact gameplay values, duration, activation button, damage multiplier, and meter rules are NOT finalized in this document.

==================================================
7. STORY IMAGE 7 - MARCH TOWARD BATTLE
==================================================

Scene:
- Jamber moves forward toward the combat area
- Dark alley / stage entrance remains visible
- Strong final pre-gameplay composition

Purpose:
- Final story transition before the tutorial and Stage 1.

==================================================
8. LOADING DURING STORY
==================================================

The story sequence can hide or reduce perceived loading time.

Possible assets to load while the player is reading the story:
- Punk textures / animations
- Hooligan textures / animations
- Gangster textures / animations
- Stage 1 backgrounds
- Enemy sounds
- Stage music
- Combat sound effects
- Enemy data / attack assets
- Tutorial assets
- Other Stage 1 resources not required on the title screen

Recommended behavior:
- Do not load everything on the title screen if it causes a long initial wait.
- Load only what is required to show the title screen first.
- Begin loading Stage 1 content after ENTER is pressed.
- Continue loading while story images are being viewed.
- Before leaving the final story/tutorial sequence, confirm required Stage 1 assets are ready.

IMPORTANT:
The loading implementation will be coded later. This document defines the intended flow only.
