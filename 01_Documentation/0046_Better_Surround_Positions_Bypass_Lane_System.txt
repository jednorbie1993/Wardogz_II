WARDOGZ II
0046 - BETTER SURROUND POSITIONS / BYPASS LANE SYSTEM
=====================================================

PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------
Prevent a Punk that is behind another Punk from endlessly
walking forward into the enemy in front.

The rear Punk should have a real chance to reach the
player and attack.

FINAL BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------
When a Punk detects another Punk blocking the direct path:

1. The rear Punk does not keep pushing straight forward.
2. The rear Punk chooses an alternate vertical lane:
   TOP or BOTTOM.
3. The Punk moves toward that bypass lane first.
4. Once the path becomes clear, normal approach toward
   the player resumes.
5. The Punk can then move into attack range.

IMPORTANT MOVEMENT RULE
--------------------------------------------------
The bypass direction is not randomly changed every frame.

The Punk keeps the chosen side long enough to move around
the blocking enemy, avoiding unstable left-right or
top-bottom shaking.

STAGE BOUNDARY SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------
The selected bypass position is kept inside the valid
stage walk area so the Punk does not attempt to move
outside the top or bottom boundary.

INTERACTION WITH 0045
--------------------------------------------------
0045 remains active.

Example:

Punk 1 -> attacks
       -> dash back
       -> short stand pause

Punk 2 -> if blocked
       -> moves TOP or BOTTOM
       -> gets a clearer lane
       -> approaches player
       -> gets a better chance to attack

RESULT
--------------------------------------------------
Multiple enemies no longer behave like one straight line.

Enemies can begin to spread vertically and create a more
natural beat-'em-up surround pattern around the player.
