WARDOGZ II
0047 - Y-DEPTH DRAW SORTING SYSTEM
==================================

PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------
Fix sprite overlap so characters at lower screen depth
are drawn above characters positioned farther toward the
top of the stage.

FINAL BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------
Characters are sorted by their ground / foot position,
not by sprite size or full image rectangle.

General draw order:

TOP
  ->
MIDDLE
  ->
BOTTOM

The character with the larger / lower foot Y position is
drawn later and therefore appears in front.

PLAYER + ENEMY SORTING
--------------------------------------------------
The player and Punk enemies use the same depth reference.

Depth reference:

    center Y of the character foot marker

This keeps the player and enemies consistent when moving
between top, middle, and bottom stage areas.

EXAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------
Player at TOP
Punk at MIDDLE

Result:
Punk appears in front of the player.

Player at BOTTOM
Punk at MIDDLE

Result:
Player appears in front of the Punk.

IMPORTANT
--------------------------------------------------
0047 is a rendering / draw-order system only.

It does not prevent characters from physically passing
through each other. Body collision is handled separately
by 0048.

RESULT
--------------------------------------------------
Sprite overlap now follows beat-'em-up depth correctly
and gives a clearer sense of foreground/background.
