0043 - DYNAMIC ATTACK SLOT SWAP / TAKEOVER SYSTEM
=================================================

DATE:
2026-08-13

SYSTEM:
Dynamic Attack Slot Swap / Takeover for Multi-Punk Combat

=================================================
GOAL
=================================================

Allow nearby waiting Punk enemies to take over an attack slot while
keeping the existing multi-enemy combat readable.

The system works together with:

    0040 - Multi-Punk / Anti-Overlap
    0041 - Attack Slot / Turn System
    0042 - Soft Surround / Reposition AI

Final current target:

    MAX ACTIVE ATTACKERS = 2

=================================================
FINAL 0043 BEHAVIOR
=================================================

Current combat setup:

- 4 Punk enemies may exist on screen.
- Maximum 2 Punk enemies may hold attack slots.
- The 2 selected attackers may chase and attack the player.
- The other Punk enemies remain in waiting / surround behavior.
- Attackers are not permanently fixed.
- When the player moves close to a waiting Punk, that Punk may take
  over one of the two attack slots.
- A farther non-attacking slot owner may lose its slot and return to
  surround / waiting behavior.
- A Punk already performing an attack keeps its slot until the current
  attack animation finishes.
- This prevents Punch / Elbow animations from being interrupted by
  slot swapping.

=================================================
EXAMPLE
=================================================

Before:

    Punk 1 = ATTACKER
    Punk 2 = ATTACKER
    Punk 3 = WAITING
    Punk 4 = WAITING

If the player moves near Punk 3:

    Punk 1 = WAITING
    Punk 2 = ATTACKER
    Punk 3 = ATTACKER
    Punk 4 = WAITING

The exact Punk that gives up its slot depends on distance and current
attack state.

Maximum active attackers remains:

    2

=================================================
TAKEOVER AREA
=================================================

Current 0043 takeover tuning:

    Horizontal distance:
        190 px

    Depth distance:
        95 px

A waiting Punk inside this approximate area receives stronger priority
to become one of the active attackers.

Current tuning values in the slot resolver:

    takeoverDistanceX = 190.0f
    takeoverDepth     = 95.0f

=================================================
SLOT STABILITY
=================================================

The system includes a small hold bonus for current attack-slot owners.

Purpose:

- prevent attack slots from changing every frame
- reduce flickering between nearly equal enemies
- make the current attackers feel stable
- still allow a nearby waiting Punk to take over when appropriate

Current concept:

    currentOwnerHoldBonus = 25.0f
    waitingTakeoverBonus  = 140.0f

Lower final score receives higher slot priority.

=================================================
ATTACK COMMITMENT
=================================================

An enemy already inside an active Punch / Elbow animation cannot lose
its attack slot halfway through the attack.

Concept:

    if (enemy.isAlive && enemy.isAttacking)
    {
        keep attack slot
    }

After the attack finishes, the slot may be reassigned normally.

This keeps compatibility with:

- Punch animation
- Elbow animation
- Elbow Frame 3 forward lunge
- attack damage / hitbox timing
- attack cooldown

=================================================
FINAL ATTACKER LIMIT DECISION
=================================================

A test was made with:

    MAX ACTIVE ATTACKERS = 3

The result was visually too crowded / aggressive for the current
character and sprite size.

Final decision:

    MAX ACTIVE ATTACKERS = 2

Reason:

- cleaner screen
- easier to read enemy attacks
- waiting enemies remain useful around the player
- better fit for the large character sprites
- preserves the street-fight surround feeling without overcrowding

=================================================
IMPORTANT ATTACK FLOW NOTE
=================================================

During the next 0044 experiment, an extra attack permission flag caused
all Punk enemies to approach the player but remain standing without
starting an attack.

The working 0043 setup keeps the normal attack flow open.

Current working behavior must allow attack start when the Punk:

- owns an attack slot
- is inside attack range
- has finished its own cooldown
- is aligned with the player
- is alive and detects the player

Do not add another attack gate unless it is initialized and updated
correctly.
