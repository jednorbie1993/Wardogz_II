WARDOGZ II
0049 - DYNAMIC ENEMY HP HUD SYSTEM
==================================

PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------
Move enemy HP information away from the enemy sprite and
into a clean arcade-style HUD.

FINAL HUD POSITION
--------------------------------------------------
Enemy HP rows are displayed at:

    TOP-RIGHT

Rows are stacked downward.

Example:

PUNK  [##########]
PUNK  [######----]
PUNK  [###-------]

DISPLAY CONTENT
--------------------------------------------------
Each active enemy row contains:

- Enemy name
- HP bar

Numeric HP values such as:

    78 / 100

are not displayed in normal gameplay.

This keeps the HUD cleaner and more arcade-like.

DYNAMIC ENEMY ROWS
--------------------------------------------------
Only relevant enemies are shown.

A new enemy HP row does not immediately appear while the
enemy is still off-screen / entering.

The row appears only after the enemy has entered the
active stage area.

DEATH BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------
When an enemy reaches 0 HP:

- The empty/depleted HP row remains visible briefly.
- Death HUD hold duration:

    1.50 seconds

- After 1.50 seconds, the row disappears.
- Remaining enemy rows move upward automatically.

When a new enemy later becomes active, a new HP row can
appear again.

SPRITE HP BAR REMOVAL
--------------------------------------------------
The old HP bar drawn directly above each Punk sprite was
removed.

Enemy HP is now represented only by the top-right HUD.

MAX ENEMY DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------
The layout is intended to support the planned maximum of
approximately 6 active enemies.

RESULT
--------------------------------------------------
The game HUD is cleaner, easier to read, and closer to a
classic arcade beat-'em-up presentation.
