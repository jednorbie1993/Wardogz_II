WARDOGZ II
0055 - STAGE 1 MULTI-BACKGROUND SYSTEM
======================================

PROJECT:
Wardogz II

SYSTEM ID:
0055

TITLE:
Stage 1 Multi-Background System


PURPOSE
-------
Replaces the temporary repeated background with multiple unique Stage 1 alley
background panels.

This turns Stage 1 into a visually progressing street / alley environment.


FINAL BACKGROUND ORDER
----------------------
The Stage 1 background sequence is:

    B1
    B2
    BA3
    BA4
    BA5
    BA6
    BA7
    BA8
    BA9
    BA10


BACKGROUND FOLDER
-----------------
The files are stored under:

    assets/background/


BACKGROUND STYLE
----------------
The Stage 1 environment follows a consistent theme:

- dark urban alley
- industrial brick buildings
- warehouse areas
- pipes
- warning signs
- dumpsters
- fences
- graffiti
- red warning lights
- cracked street / pavement
- open foreground for beat-'em-up movement


BACKGROUND PANEL SIZE
---------------------
Most Stage 1 background panels use approximately:

    1672 x 941

BA10 is intentionally much narrower.

BA10 is used as a short final extension / ending piece instead of being
stretched to the same width as the other panels.


WHY BA10 IS NOT STRETCHED
-------------------------
Stretching BA10 to the full width of the other images would distort:

- wall details
- props
- pavement
- lighting
- perspective

Instead, BA10 keeps its proportional width and acts as the final stage strip.


RENDER SCALE
------------
The backgrounds are scaled to match the gameplay height while preserving their
aspect ratio.

The system does NOT force every background panel to have an identical world
width.

Each panel contributes its own scaled width to the stage.


STAGE LENGTH
------------
Originally, Stage 1 was planned around approximately:

    8000 pixels

However, after adding all prepared background panels, forcing them into 8000
pixels would compress or distort the artwork.

The final approach is:

    total stage width =
        sum of the scaled widths of all valid background panels


ADVANTAGE
---------
This allows:

- no horizontal stretching
- consistent image proportions
- natural stage length
- easier background replacement
- easy addition/removal of future sections


SEAM TESTING
------------
Before applying the full Stage 1 sequence, B1 and B2 were tested first.

The purpose was to verify:

- matching source dimensions
- matching ground height
- matching wall height
- transition between panels
- camera scrolling across a seam

After the initial seam test, the rest of the panels were added.


GAMEPLAY AREA
-------------
The lower portion of each background remains mostly open for:

- player movement
- enemy movement
- combat
- depth movement
- future wave locking
