0042 - PUNK SOFT SURROUND / REPOSITION AI SYSTEM
================================================

DATE:
2026-08-12

SYSTEM:
Soft Surround / Formation AI for Multi-Punk Combat

================================================
GOAL
================================================

Make multiple Punk enemies spread around the player instead of all
chasing the exact same point.

The surround system supports the 0041 two-attacker system without
making waiting Punk enemies run away from the player.

================================================
ORIGINAL 0042 PROBLEM
================================================

The first hard-formation version used fixed left/right/upper/lower
targets too aggressively.

This caused:

- Punk enemies to look like they were avoiding the player
- waiting Punk enemies to move too far away
- active attackers sometimes failing to reach attack range
- the player having difficulty hitting waiting Punk enemies

There was also a logic deadlock:

- a Punk needed to be near enough to attack before receiving a slot
- but a Punk without a slot was being forced toward a formation point
- therefore some enemies never approached enough to become attackers

This behavior was replaced with Soft Surround AI.

================================================
FINAL SOFT SURROUND BEHAVIOR
================================================

Current design:

- 4 Punk enemies may surround the player.
- 2 selected Punk enemies receive attack slots.
- Attack-slot Punk enemies ignore formation pressure and chase the player normally.
- Waiting Punk enemies stay nearby instead of running away.
- Formation positions are guidance only.
- They are not hard destinations.
- Waiting Punk enemies remain close enough to be reached and hit.
- When attack-slot ownership changes, the new attacker leaves its waiting position and chases the player.
- When a Punk loses its attack slot, it may return to nearby surround behavior.

================================================
SURROUND POSITIONS
================================================

The system uses approximate relative positions around the player.

Current soft offsets are approximately:

    LEFT:
        X = -190

    RIGHT:
        X = +190

    UPPER / BACK DEPTH:
        Y = -80

    LOWER / FRONT DEPTH:
        Y = +80

These positions move with the player.

They are not strict coordinates that a Punk must continuously reach.

================================================
ATTACKERS VS WAITING PUNKS
================================================

ATTACK-SLOT PUNK:

- normal chase behavior
- moves toward the player
- enters Punch / Elbow range
- may attack
- formation does not pull it away during active pressure

WAITING PUNK:

- remains nearby
- can reposition softly
- does not run away when the player approaches
- remains targetable by player attacks
- may become an attacker when attack-slot priority changes

================================================
SOFT HOLD DISTANCE
================================================

Waiting Punk enemies stop forcing formation movement once they are
already reasonably near the player.

Current approximate nearby limits:

    Horizontal:
        about 260 px

    Depth:
        about 115 px

Inside this area:

- the Punk may hold position
- anti-overlap can still separate enemies
- the Punk remains reachable
- the Punk does not retreat just to satisfy formation

================================================
ATTACK SLOT DEADLOCK FIX
================================================

0042 also fixes the interaction with 0041.

Instead of requiring enemies to already be inside attack range before
receiving an attack slot:

    suitable / nearby Punk enemies may receive attack slots first

Then:

    attack slot
        ->
    normal chase
        ->
    attack range
        ->
    Punch / Elbow

This prevents waiting enemies from getting permanently stuck outside
attack range.

================================================
ANTI-OVERLAP COMPATIBILITY
================================================

0040 anti-overlap remains active.

Soft Surround and Anti-Overlap have different jobs:

Soft Surround:
- controls general position around player

Anti-Overlap:
- prevents Punk enemies from occupying the same space

The systems work together.
