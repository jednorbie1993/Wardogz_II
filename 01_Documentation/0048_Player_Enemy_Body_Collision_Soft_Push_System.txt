WARDOGZ II
0048 - PLAYER / ENEMY BODY COLLISION + SOFT PUSH SYSTEM
=======================================================

PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------
Prevent the player from simply passing through Punk
enemies when standing at similar depth.

The system adds physical presence without creating a
heavy knockback effect.

FINAL BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------
- Player and enemy body contact is checked near the
  ground / foot area.
- When their body contact areas overlap at similar depth,
  a small separation correction is applied.
- The player can still move around enemies by going
  toward the top or bottom.
- Directly walking through an enemy is no longer allowed
  as easily.

SOFT PUSH
--------------------------------------------------
The collision response is intentionally gentle.

Normal contact:
- Player moves slightly away.
- Enemy also moves slightly away.

This avoids a rigid wall feeling.

PLANTED ENEMY STATES
--------------------------------------------------
When a Punk is:

- attacking,
- performing dash-back retreat, or
- standing during the post-retreat pause,

the Punk behaves as more planted.

During these states:
- more of the separation correction is applied to the
  player,
- less movement is applied to the Punk.

This protects attack, retreat, and pause animations from
being visually disrupted.

INTERACTION WITH DEPTH
--------------------------------------------------
0048 works together with 0047.

0047:
Controls who is drawn in front.

0048:
Controls physical body contact.

RESULT
--------------------------------------------------
Characters now have more believable physical presence
while still allowing free top/bottom beat-'em-up movement.
