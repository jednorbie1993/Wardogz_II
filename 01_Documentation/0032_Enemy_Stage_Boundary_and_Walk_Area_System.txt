WARDOGZ II DOCUMENTATION
0032 - Enemy Stage Boundary / Walk Area System

Date: 2026-08-11

============================================================
TITLE
============================================================

0032_Enemy_Stage_Boundary_and_Walk_Area_System

============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

Prevent the Punk enemy from leaving the normal combat walk area
while using the 0031 chase AI.

Before 0032:
- Punk could chase the player upward/downward without a stage Y clamp.
- Punk only had horizontal screen protection.

After 0032:
- Punk stays inside the same logical walkAreaTop / walkAreaBottom
  used by the player.
- Horizontal chase and knockback remain inside the screen.
- Vertical/depth chase is clamped to the normal combat area.

============================================================
NEW ENEMY DATA
============================================================

float stageAnchorOffsetY;

Purpose:
Converts enemy hurtbox.y into the enemy's logical stage Y.

For Punk:
stageAnchorOffsetY = 200.0f

Because InitPunk(x, y) creates the hurtbox at:

y - 200.0f

============================================================
NEW INTERNAL SYSTEM
============================================================

ClampEnemyToStage()

It limits:
- LEFT  = 0
- RIGHT = screenWidth - hurtbox.width
- TOP   = walkAreaTop
- BOTTOM = walkAreaBottom

The Y clamp uses the enemy stage anchor, not the top of the tall
body hurtbox.

============================================================
IMPORTANT FOR FUTURE ENEMY INTRO
============================================================

0032 is the NORMAL COMBAT boundary system.

A future enemy entrance/spawn system can temporarily bypass the
normal clamp so an enemy can intentionally enter from:
- top
- left
- right
- other off-stage spawn points

Once the entrance finishes, normal 0032 boundaries can be enabled.
