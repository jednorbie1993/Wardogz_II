WARDOGZ II
0044 - ENEMY ATTACK COOLDOWN / TURN TIMING SYSTEM
==================================================

PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------
Prevent multiple active Punk enemies from starting
their attacks at exactly the same time.

The system gives enemies a short shared delay between
attack starts so combat does not look like attack spam.

FINAL BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------
- Two Punk enemies can still approach and stay active.
- Only one Punk should begin an attack first.
- After an attack begins, the other Punk must wait for
  a short attack-start gap.
- The current shared attack-start gap is approximately:

    0.55 seconds

- Existing Punch and Elbow attack sequences are preserved.

EXAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------
Punk 1 -> starts attack
          |
          | 0.55 sec attack-start gap
          v
Punk 2 -> allowed to start attack

IMPORTANT
--------------------------------------------------
0044 controls attack START timing only.

It does not automatically force enemies to move far away
from each other. Visual separation between attackers is
handled by later movement systems such as 0045 and 0046.

RESULT
--------------------------------------------------
Enemy attacks feel less simultaneous and provide a base
for alternating enemy pressure around the player.
