WARDOGZ II
0045 - ENEMY DASH-BACK RETREAT / PAUSE SYSTEM
==============================================

PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------
After a Punk finishes an attack, he should not remain
stuck directly beside the player.

Instead, the Punk performs a quick backward dash, then
briefly holds position before approaching again.

FINAL BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------
Attack
  ->
Quick dash backward
  ->
Stand / stance pause
  ->
Resume normal AI approach

CURRENT TEST VALUES
--------------------------------------------------
Dash / retreat duration:

    0.24f seconds

Dash / retreat speed:

    480.0f

Approximate dash distance:

    about 115 px

Post-retreat stand pause:

    0.55f seconds

FACING BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------
While performing the backward dash, the Punk keeps the
same combat-facing direction instead of instantly turning
around.

This makes the retreat look more like a defensive
back-dash instead of normal walking away.

ENEMY PUSH PROTECTION
--------------------------------------------------
While a Punk is:

- actively retreating, or
- standing in the post-retreat pause

the other Punk should not push that Punk out of position.

This protects the visual pause after the dash.

RESULT
--------------------------------------------------
The enemy now creates space after attacking instead of
immediately staying glued to the player or instantly
walking forward again.
