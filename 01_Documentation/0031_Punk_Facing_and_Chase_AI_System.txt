0031 - Punk Facing and Chase AI System

Date: 2026-08-11

============================================================
TITLE
============================================================

0031_Punk_Facing_and_Chase_AI_System

============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

Make the Stage 1 Punk actively follow the player instead of
waiting as a stationary enemy.

0031 adds:
- automatic enemy facing
- left/right sprite mirroring
- horizontal chase movement
- vertical/depth chase movement
- attack-range stopping
- diagonal chase speed normalization

============================================================
ENEMY BEHAVIOR
============================================================

PLAYER FAR AWAY:
Punk moves toward the player.

PLAYER ON DIFFERENT DEPTH / GROUND Y:
Punk moves up or down until the foot markers are close enough.

PLAYER ON THE LEFT:
Punk faces LEFT.

PLAYER ON THE RIGHT:
Punk faces RIGHT.

PLAYER INSIDE ATTACK POSITION:
Punk stops chasing and the existing 0030 attack system can attack.

Basic flow:

IDLE / TRACK PLAYER
        |
        v
CHASE PLAYER
        |
        v
ALIGN DEPTH
        |
        v
STOP IN ATTACK RANGE
        |
        v
0030 BASIC ATTACK

============================================================
NEW ENEMY DATA
============================================================

bool facingRight;
- false = facing LEFT
- true  = facing RIGHT

bool isChasing;
- true while the enemy is currently moving toward the player

float chaseSpeed;
- Punk movement speed
- current Punk value: 115.0f

float chaseStopDistance;
- horizontal distance where the Punk stops advancing
- current Punk value: 155.0f

float chaseDepthTolerance;
- allowed Y/depth difference before the Punk stops moving vertically
- current Punk value: 8.0f

============================================================
SPRITE FACING
============================================================

No new Punk image is required.

The existing idle sprite is mirrored horizontally depending on
which side of the Punk the player is standing on.

The current Punk art is treated as facing LEFT by default.

facingRight = false
- draw normal source rectangle

facingRight = true
- draw mirrored source rectangle

============================================================
CHASE MOVEMENT
============================================================

The Punk compares its center position with the player.

Horizontal:
- player right of Punk -> move right
- player left of Punk  -> move left

Depth / Y:
- player foot marker below Punk -> move down
- player foot marker above Punk -> move up

If both X and Y movement happen together, diagonal movement is
normalized with approximately:

0.70710678f

This prevents diagonal chase movement from becoming faster than
straight movement.

============================================================
0030 SYSTEMS PRESERVED
============================================================

0031 keeps the existing:

- Player HP
- Player hurtbox
- Enemy hurtbox
- Enemy attack hitbox
- Player -> Enemy damage
- Enemy -> Player damage
- Knockback
- Hit reaction
- Foot / ground markers
- Ground-depth hit validation
- Enemy attack cooldown

The Punk does not chase while:
- attacking
- receiving hit reaction
- dead

============================================================
TEST
============================================================

1. Build and run Wardogz II.

2. Move to the LEFT side of Punk.
Expected:
- Punk faces left.
- Punk moves toward player.

3. Move to the RIGHT side of Punk.
Expected:
- Punk sprite mirrors and faces right.
- Punk moves toward player.

4. Move UP or DOWN away from Punk's ground depth.
Expected:
- Punk follows vertically.
- Punk tries to align its foot marker with the player's foot marker.

5. Move diagonally away.
Expected:
- Punk follows diagonally.
- diagonal movement should not be faster than straight movement.

6. Let Punk reach attack range.
Expected:
- Punk stops advancing.
- existing 0030 enemy attack activates.
- player can still receive damage and knockback.

7. Attack Punk while it chases.
Expected:
- Punk stops chasing during hit reaction.
- player attack damage and knockback still work.

8. Reduce Punk HP to 0.
Expected:
- Punk stops chasing and attacking.
