WARDOGZ II - DOCUMENTATION 0080
WAVE 1 - EARLY HOOLIGAN APPROACH + 17% FORWARD GATE

PURPOSE
- Avoid the enemy suddenly appearing only when Jamber reaches a fight trigger.
- Enemy should already be farther ahead in the map and visibly run toward the player.
- The forward gate is separate from the enemy activation/spawn position.

CURRENT WAVE 1 FLOW
1. Jamber reaches about 2% of Stage 1.
2. Wave 1 activates.
3. Hooligan starts at about 9% of the map.
4. Hooligan runs left toward about 5.5%.
5. Jamber can continue moving/fighting.
6. Jamber cannot pass 17% while the Hooligan is alive.
7. Kill the Hooligan.
8. 17% gate unlocks.
9. Jamber can continue through the stage.

TUNING VALUES
#define WAVE_1_ACTIVATE_PROGRESS 0.02f
#define WAVE_1_SPAWN_PROGRESS 0.09f
#define WAVE_1_ENTRANCE_TARGET_PROGRESS 0.055f
#define WAVE_1_FIGHT_BOUNDARY_PROGRESS 0.17f

MEANING
- ACTIVATE_PROGRESS
  When the wave becomes active based on Jamber's map progress.

- SPAWN_PROGRESS
  Where the enemy begins in the stage.

- ENTRANCE_TARGET_PROGRESS
  Where the enemy initially runs toward before normal AI/chase takes over.

- FIGHT_BOUNDARY_PROGRESS
  Maximum forward progress allowed while the wave is alive.

ENTRANCE SPEED
StartEnemyEntrance(
    &enemies[0],
    entranceTargetX,
    spawnStageY,
    230.0f
);

Current:
- 230.0f = entrance/run speed.

Examples:
- 180.0f = slower.
- 230.0f = current.
- 300.0f = faster.

ENEMY TYPE
Current:
InitHooligan(...)

Can later be changed to:
- InitPunk(...)
- InitGangster(...)

MULTIPLE ENEMIES
For another enemy:
- Use a different enemy slot, e.g. enemies[1].
- Initialize its type and position.
- Call StartEnemyEntrance() for that slot.
- Increase:
  waves->activeEnemyCount

Example:
waves->activeEnemyCount = 2;

SPAWN Y / LANE
Current Wave 1 uses the middle of:
walkAreaTop and walkAreaBottom.

Future waves can spawn enemies in upper, middle, or lower lanes to avoid stacking and make entrances look more natural.

DESIGN RULE GOING FORWARD
- Enemy should usually appear/approach before the forward gate becomes relevant.
- Avoid obvious teleport spawning inside the visible screen.
- Reinforcements should use off-screen or ahead-of-player entrances whenever possible.
