WARDOGZ II
0069 - ENEMY ATTACK HIT SOUND EFFECTS
============================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Plays a punch sound only when an enemy attack successfully hits and damages
the player.

The sound must not play when:

- the enemy only starts an attack
- the enemy attack misses
- the player is outside the hitbox
- the player is already dead


SOUND FILES
-----------
The two sound files are:

    assets/sfx/punch.wav
    assets/sfx/punch1.wav

The sounds alternate after every successful enemy hit:

    first successful hit  -> punch.wav
    second successful hit -> punch1.wav
    third successful hit  -> punch.wav
    fourth successful hit -> punch1.wav

This prevents every impact from sounding exactly the same.


IMPORTANT HIT RULE
------------------
The sound is played inside:

    DamagePlayer()

File:

    src/player/player.c

DamagePlayer() is called only after the enemy attack hitbox connects with the
player. This is why the sound matches the real impact instead of the attack
animation start.

The system applies to Vargas and any other enemy that damages the player
through the shared DamagePlayer() function.


PLAYER AUDIO DATA
-----------------
The following fields were added to Player in:

    include/player/player.h

Fields:

    Sound enemyHitSound;
    Sound enemyHitSoundAlternate;
    bool useAlternateEnemyHitSound;

Meaning:

    enemyHitSound          -> punch.wav
    enemyHitSoundAlternate -> punch1.wav
    useAlternate...        -> chooses which sound plays next


LOADING THE SOUNDS
------------------
The sounds are loaded inside InitPlayer():

    player.enemyHitSound =
        LoadSound("assets/sfx/punch.wav");

    player.enemyHitSoundAlternate =
        LoadSound("assets/sfx/punch1.wav");

    player.useAlternateEnemyHitSound = false;


PLAYING THE SOUND
-----------------
After HP damage is accepted, DamagePlayer() selects one sound:

    Sound hitSound =
        player->useAlternateEnemyHitSound
        ? player->enemyHitSoundAlternate
        : player->enemyHitSound;

It plays only when the sound loaded successfully:

    if (hitSound.frameCount > 0)
    {
        PlaySound(hitSound);
    }

The alternate flag then switches for the next successful hit.


AUDIO DEVICE ORDER
------------------
The audio device must be initialized before InitPlayer() loads the sounds.

Correct startup order in src/main.c:

    InitWindow(...);
    InitAudioDevice();
    SetTargetFPS(60);
    Player player = InitPlayer(...);

Correct shutdown order:

    UnloadPlayer(&player);
    CloseAudioDevice();
    CloseWindow();


UNLOADING
---------
UnloadPlayer() releases both Sound objects when their frameCount is valid.
This prevents audio-resource leaks during shutdown.


MANUAL ADJUSTMENT GUIDE
-----------------------
To replace the sounds without changing the C code, keep the same filenames:

    punch.wav
    punch1.wav

Place the replacement files inside:

    assets/sfx/

Recommended format:

- WAV
- short impact sound
- no long silence at the beginning
- similar volume between both files

3. src/main.c
