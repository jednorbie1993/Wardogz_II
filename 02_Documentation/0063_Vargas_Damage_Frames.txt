0063 - VARGAS DAMAGE FRAME RULES
============================================================

Boss Punch:

- Four animation frames.
- Visible Frame 2 deals damage.
- Visible Frame 3 deals damage.
- The two frames represent the left and right punches.
- No Frame 1 freeze.
- No forward slide.

Boss Kick:

- Four animation frames.
- Visible Frame 3 deals damage only.
- No Frame 1 freeze.
- No forward slide.
- The Punk Elbow freeze/slide logic is blocked for Vargas.

Boss Combo:

- Existing separate Combo impact frames remain active.
- Visible Frames 2 and 4 deal damage.

Boss Knee:

- Visible Frame 3 deals damage.

Boss Uppercut:

- Visible Frame 3 deals damage.

Boss Heavy Blow:

- Existing Heavy Blow impact behavior remains unchanged.

The attackHitFrames bit mask allows Vargas Punch and Combo to damage the
player on more than one animation frame without applying repeated damage on
the same frame.

Files involved:

- src/enemy/enemy_attack.c
- src/enemy/enemy_draw.c

============================================================
