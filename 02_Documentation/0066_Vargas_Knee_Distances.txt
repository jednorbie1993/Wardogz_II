0066 - INDIVIDUAL KNEE ATTACK AND STOP DISTANCES
============================================================

Boss Knee received its own attack range and stop distance instead of always
using the Boss Punch values.

Required Enemy fields in include/enemy/enemy.h:

    float bossKneeAttackRange;
    float bossKneeStopDistance;

Default initialization in src/enemy/enemy.c:

    enemy.bossKneeAttackRange = 0.0f;
    enemy.bossKneeStopDistance = 0.0f;

Current Vargas tuning in src/enemy/stage_1/boss.c:

    enemy.bossKneeAttackRange = 405.0f;
    enemy.bossKneeStopDistance = 400.0f;
    enemy.bossKneeLungeDistance = 300.0f;

Meaning:

- bossKneeAttackRange: maximum horizontal distance allowed for Knee attack.
- bossKneeStopDistance: distance at which Vargas stops approaching for Knee.
- bossKneeLungeDistance: forward movement during Knee Frames 2-3.

Knee attack range selection was added to GetNextEnemyAttackRange() in:

    src/enemy/enemy_attack.c

Knee stop distance selection was added to GetNextEnemyAttackStopDistance() in:

    src/enemy/enemy_move.c

Files involved:

- include/enemy/enemy.h
- src/enemy/enemy.c
- src/enemy/enemy_attack.c
- src/enemy/enemy_move.c
- src/enemy/stage_1/boss.c
