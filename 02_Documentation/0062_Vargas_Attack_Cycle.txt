0062 - VARGAS COMPLETE ATTACK CYCLE
============================================================

The Vargas attack cycle was updated so every available move is used before
the sequence repeats.

Final attack order:

1. Boss Punch
2. Boss Kick
3. Boss Combo
4. Boss Knee
5. Boss Uppercut
6. Boss Heavy Blow
7. Return to Boss Punch

Internal attack mapping:

ENEMY_ATTACK_PUNCH             = Boss Punch
ENEMY_ATTACK_ELBOW             = Boss Kick
ENEMY_ATTACK_BOSS_COMBO        = Boss Combo
ENEMY_ATTACK_BOSS_KNEE         = Boss Knee
ENEMY_ATTACK_BOSS_UPPERCUT     = Boss Uppercut
ENEMY_ATTACK_BOSS_HEAVY_BLOW   = Boss Heavy Blow

Note:
The internal ELBOW slot is reused by Vargas for the Boss Kick animation.
This does not mean Vargas performs the Punk Elbow move.

- src/enemy/enemy_attack.c
- src/enemy/stage_1/boss.c
