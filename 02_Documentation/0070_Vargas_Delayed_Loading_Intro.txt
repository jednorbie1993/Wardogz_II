WARDOGZ II
0070 - VARGAS DELAYED LOADING / BOSS INTRO
============================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Prevents all Vargas combat textures from loading during normal game startup.
The heavy Boss loading is delayed until the player reaches 86 percent of
Stage 1.

This makes the executable reach normal gameplay sooner and hides the one-time
Boss texture loading behind a cinematic overlay.


INITIAL 0070 TRIGGER VALUES
---------------------------
The system was added in:

    src/main.c

Initial constants:

    VARGAS_TRIGGER_PROGRESS = 0.86f
    VARGAS_WORLD_X = 11452.0f
    VARGAS_STAGE_Y = 540.0f
    VARGAS_INTRO_LOAD_DELAY = 0.35f
    VARGAS_INTRO_DURATION = 3.20f

Meaning:

- The loading intro starts when the player reaches 86 percent of the stage.
- Vargas is created at world X 11452, near 98 percent of the stage.
- The first version placed him in the lower/middle lane at Y 540.
- The overlay appears before InitBoss() begins loading all Boss textures.
- The first version ended automatically after 3.20 seconds.

The Y position and automatic ending were changed later in 0071.


DYNAMIC STAGE TRIGGER
---------------------
The trigger uses the real calculated Stage 1 width:

    vargasTriggerX =
        stageWorldWidth * VARGAS_TRIGGER_PROGRESS;

This avoids hard-coding the 86-percent trigger to one exact background width.


DELAYED TEXTURE LOADING
-----------------------
At program startup, only this single portrait texture is loaded:

    boss_battle_idle1.png

The complete Vargas texture set is not loaded until the intro has already
covered the screen.

After the overlay has been visible for 0.35 seconds:

    enemies[0] = InitBoss(VARGAS_WORLD_X, VARGAS_STAGE_Y);

InitBoss() performs the one-time loading of Vargas idle, battle idle, run,
attack, dash, and death textures.


GAMEPLAY PAUSE
--------------
While the Vargas intro is active:

- player controls are paused
- enemy AI is paused
- Vargas is not exposed before loading finishes
- the cinematic is drawn in screen space

deltaTime is capped during the loading intro so a long synchronous loading
frame cannot skip the entire cinematic.


INITIAL CINEMATIC DESIGN
------------------------
The first 0070 version used:

- dark black overlay
- red scanlines
- red vertical accent line
- Vargas portrait
- warning text
- Vargas title reveal
- animated red loading pulse

The exact dialogue and Continue behavior were replaced by the improved 0071
multi-page intro.


TEMPORARY PORTRAIT CLEANUP
--------------------------
The startup portrait is a separate temporary Texture2D.

After the Boss intro ends, it is unloaded because the Vargas shared texture
cache already owns its normal battle-idle textures.

A shutdown safety check also unloads the portrait if the program closes before
the intro finishes.


INITIAL DRAW-VISIBILITY PROBLEM
-------------------------------
The first implementation accidentally used the same 500-pixel proximity
condition for both:

- Vargas body drawing
- Vargas HP HUD visibility

Result:

Vargas could disappear visually when the player moved back below about 94
percent, even though his AI and attacks continued running.

This was a drawing-condition problem, not an AI or texture problem.
It was corrected in 0071 by separating body drawing from HUD visibility.


FILES UPDATED
-------------
1. src/main.c

The previously completed 0068 enrage system and 0069 audio initialization
remain active in this version.
