WARDOGZ II - DOCUMENTATION 0076
CAMERA 25%-75% SCREEN DEAD ZONE

PURPOSE
- Palitan ang old camera behavior na laging naka-center kay Jamber.
- Camera movement is based on the visible game screen, not stage/map percentage.

BEHAVIOR
- 0%-25% screen area: camera can move left when Jamber reaches/passes the left threshold.
- 25%-75% screen area: camera remains steady.
- 75%-100% screen area: camera moves right when Jamber reaches/passes the right threshold.
- Jamber is no longer forced to remain around the 50% center of the screen.

MAIN SETTINGS
float leftScreenLimit = screenWidth * 0.25f;
float rightScreenLimit = screenWidth * 0.75f;

ADJUSTMENT
- 0.20f / 0.80f = wider free movement area.
- 0.30f / 0.70f = camera follows sooner.
- Current target = 0.25f / 0.75f.

WORLD CLAMP
- Camera must still stay inside Stage 1.
- Minimum camera target X = half screen width.
- Maximum camera target X = stageWorldWidth - half screen width.
- Prevents black/empty area from appearing before or after the background.

FILES
- src/main.c

NOTES
- Horizontal camera only was changed.
- Y camera behavior remains fixed to the stage view.
