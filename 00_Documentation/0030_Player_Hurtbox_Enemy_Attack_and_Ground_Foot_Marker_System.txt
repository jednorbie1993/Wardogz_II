0030 - Player Hurtbox + Enemy Attack + Ground Foot Marker System

Date: 2026-08-11

============================================================
TITLE
============================================================

0030_Player_Hurtbox_Enemy_Attack_and_Ground_Foot_Marker_System

============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

Make combat two-way.

Before 0030:
The player could attack and damage the Punk enemy.

After 0030:
The Punk can attack the player when the player enters attack range.

============================================================
NEW PLAYER SYSTEMS
============================================================

Player HP:
100

Player hurtbox:
Follows the visible/scaled player body.

Debug color:
GREEN

Player ground / foot marker:
A small marker at the player's feet represents the player's
actual position on the stage depth axis.

Debug color:
PURPLE

Player hit reaction:
When hit, the player receives damage and short knockback.

============================================================
NEW ENEMY ATTACK
============================================================

Enemy attack damage:
10

Enemy attack range:
210

Enemy attack active time:
0.18 seconds

Enemy attack cooldown:
1.10 seconds after an attack

Enemy attack knockback:
190

Enemy hit reaction applied to player:
0.16 seconds

Enemy attack hitbox debug color:
YELLOW

Enemy ground / foot marker:
A small marker at the enemy's feet represents the enemy's
actual position on the stage depth axis.

Debug color:
ORANGE

============================================================
HOW IT WORKS
============================================================

1. Punk checks horizontal distance to the player.
2. Player and enemy ground/foot marker Y positions are checked.
3. The player and enemy must be close enough on the same ground/depth axis.
4. If the player is inside attack range and cooldown is ready,
   Punk starts a basic attack.
5. A yellow enemy attack hitbox appears.
6. A hit is valid only when BOTH conditions are true:

   A. Attack hitbox overlaps the target body hurtbox.
   B. Player and enemy foot/ground marker Y positions are within
      the allowed depth tolerance.

7. On a successful enemy hit:
   Player HP decreases by 10.
8. Player receives short knockback.
9. Punk waits for cooldown before attacking again.

============================================================
GROUND / FOOT MARKER DEPTH SYSTEM
============================================================

The full body hurtboxes are NOT used by themselves to decide
whether two characters are on the same stage depth.

This fixes the problem where tall player/enemy body boxes could
overlap even when one character was visually in the TOP area
and the other was in the MIDDLE or BOTTOM area.

PLAYER:
GREEN  = body hurtbox
PURPLE = foot / ground marker

ENEMY:
BLUE   = body hurtbox
YELLOW = attack hitbox
ORANGE = foot / ground marker

VALID HIT RULE:

Attack Hitbox overlaps Body Hurtbox
+
Player Foot Marker Y is close to Enemy Foot Marker Y
=
VALID HIT

IMPORTANT:
The foot markers do NOT need to overlap on the X axis.
Horizontal range is already handled by the attack hitbox and
body hurtbox collision.

The ground-depth system compares the CENTER Y position of the
player and enemy foot markers.

depthDistance =
absolute difference between playerGroundY and enemyGroundY

A hit is allowed only when:

depthDistance <= depthTolerance

The same ground-depth validation is applied in BOTH directions:

- Enemy attack -> Player
- Player attack -> Enemy

This fixes the bug where the player could still hit the enemy
from TOP/MIDDLE/BOTTOM differences just because the large attack
and body boxes touched.

The foot marker works like a character's ground/shadow position.
The feet represent the character's actual stage depth, not the
head, hands, or full sprite height.

============================================================
FOOT MARKER TUNING
============================================================

Default marker height:
14.0f

Foot marker WIDTH examples:

60% width:
X offset = 0.20f
Width    = 0.60f

80% width:
X offset = 0.10f
Width    = 0.80f

100% width:
X offset = 0.00f
Width    = 1.00f

120% width:
X offset = -0.10f
Width    = 1.20f

Negative X offset extends the marker farther to the left.

============================================================
DEPTH TOLERANCE
============================================================

depthTolerance controls how close the foot-marker Y positions
must be before an attack is valid.

Smaller value = stricter.
Larger value  = more forgiving.

Example values:

0.10f = extremely strict
10.0f = strict
20.0f = moderate
30.0f = more forgiving

NOTE:
Changing the visual width of the foot marker does not determine
the depth hit by itself. The final depth validation uses the
marker Y positions.

============================================================
IMPORTANT
============================================================

No new Punk attack sprite is required yet.

The Punk still shows its current idle sprite while the attack
logic/hitbox is active.

This system establishes the gameplay logic first.

A later system can add:
- Punk punch/kick animation
- enemy facing sprite
- enemy movement/chase AI
- different enemy attack data
- player hit animation
- death animation

============================================================
0029 SETTINGS PRESERVED
============================================================

COMBO_WINDOW = 2.00f
ATTACK_RECOVERY_TIME = 0.00f
CANCEL_WINDOW_FRAME = 0

These values preserve the more responsive attack feel chosen
before starting 0030.

2. Walk close to Punk.
3. Wait near Punk.

Expected:
- Yellow enemy attack hitbox appears.
- Green player hurtbox is visible.
- Purple player foot marker is visible.
- Orange enemy foot marker is visible.
- Player HP starts at 100.
- Same-depth attacks can connect.
- Different-depth attacks do NOT connect even if large body
  boxes visually overlap.
- Player -> Enemy attacks require the ground-depth check.
- Enemy -> Player attacks require the ground-depth check.
- A successful enemy hit lowers HP by 10.
- Player is pushed slightly away.
- Punk can damage the player again on a new attack.
- Punk does not damage every frame.
- Punk waits between attacks.

Player attacks should still:
- damage Punk
- use per-move damage
- use per-move knockback
- support input buffering
- support A -> W -> D combo
- support directional command detection
