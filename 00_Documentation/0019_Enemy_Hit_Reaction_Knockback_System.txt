WARDOGZ II DOCUMENTATION
0019 - Enemy Hit Reaction / Knockback System

Date: 2026-08-10

============================================================
TITLE
============================================================

0019_Enemy_Hit_Reaction_Knockback_System


============================================================
OVERVIEW
============================================================

This update adds a basic hit reaction and knockback system to
the enemy dummy introduced in Documentation 0018.

Previously, a successful player attack only reduced the enemy's
HP. The enemy now also reacts visually and moves backward when
hit.

The knockback direction depends on the player's facing direction.


============================================================
PREVIOUS SYSTEM
============================================================

0018 added:

- Enemy dummy
- Blue enemy hurtbox
- HP system
- Player attack collision detection
- One damage registration per attack

0019 builds on that system by adding physical feedback when a
successful attack connects.


============================================================
ENEMY HIT REACTION
============================================================

When the player's active attack hitbox collides with the enemy
hurtbox, the enemy enters a temporary hit reaction state.

During this reaction:

- Enemy HP decreases
- Enemy briefly changes color for visual feedback
- Enemy is pushed away from the player
- The reaction automatically ends after a short duration

This makes successful attacks easier to see and feel during
combat testing.


============================================================
KNOCKBACK DIRECTION
============================================================

The knockback direction uses the player's current facing
direction.

Player facing RIGHT:

Player  --->  Enemy
              ---> Knockback

The enemy moves to the RIGHT.

Player facing LEFT:

Knockback <---
Enemy     <--- Player

The enemy moves to the LEFT.

This works for all four current basic attacks.


============================================================
SUPPORTED ATTACKS
============================================================

The knockback system was tested with:

A = Left Punch
W = Right Punch
S = Left Kick
D = Right Kick

All four attacks correctly push the enemy in the direction the
player is attacking.


============================================================
HIT REACTION STATE
============================================================

The Enemy structure now stores temporary hit reaction data.

The system tracks whether the enemy is currently reacting to a
hit and how long the reaction should remain active.

This prevents the knockback from continuing forever and creates
a short controlled reaction after each successful attack.


============================================================
KNOCKBACK MOVEMENT
============================================================

While the enemy is in the hit reaction state, its horizontal
position changes over time.

The movement is frame-rate independent because the knockback
movement uses deltaTime.

Basic behavior:

Player attack connects
        |
        v
Enemy takes damage
        |
        v
Hit reaction begins
        |
        v
Knockback direction is selected
        |
        v
Enemy moves backward briefly
        |
        v
Reaction timer ends
        |
        v
Enemy stops moving


============================================================
VISUAL HIT FEEDBACK
============================================================

During the temporary hit reaction, the enemy dummy changes color.

This temporary color change is only development feedback.

It makes it easier to confirm that:

- Collision happened
- Damage was registered
- Hit reaction started
- Knockback was triggered

The debug visual can later be replaced with a real enemy hit
sprite or hit animation.


============================================================
ENEMY DEATH BEHAVIOR
============================================================

The existing HP system remains active.

When enemy HP reaches zero:

enemy->hp = 0;
enemy->isAlive = false;

After the enemy is no longer alive:

- No additional hit reaction occurs
- No additional knockback occurs
- The enemy stops moving

This was tested successfully.


============================================================
BOUNDARY PROTECTION
============================================================

The enemy knockback is restricted by the horizontal screen
boundaries.

This prevents repeated attacks from pushing the enemy completely
outside the playable screen.


============================================================
ONE HIT PER ATTACK
============================================================

The system still uses:

hitByCurrentAttack

This prevents one attack animation from repeatedly damaging and
knocking back the enemy every game frame.

One successful attack produces one damage event and one knockback
reaction.

After the player's attack finishes, the hit lock resets and the
next attack can hit normally.


============================================================
CURRENT COMBAT FLOW
============================================================

The current basic combat flow is now:

1. Player presses W, A, S, or D.
2. Attack animation begins.
3. Player movement is temporarily locked.
4. Attack reaches its active hit frame.
5. Red player attack hitbox appears.
6. Red hitbox overlaps the blue enemy hurtbox.
7. Collision is detected.
8. Enemy loses HP.
9. Enemy enters hit reaction state.
10. Enemy briefly changes color.
11. Enemy moves backward based on player facing direction.
12. Hit reaction ends.
13. Enemy waits for the next valid attack.

If HP reaches 0, the enemy becomes inactive and stops reacting.


============================================================
FILES INVOLVED
============================================================

include/enemy.h

    Stores the Enemy structure and hit reaction / knockback data.

src/enemy.c

    Handles damage detection, hit reaction state, knockback
    movement, enemy death behavior, and enemy drawing.

src/main.c

    Updates the enemy system every game frame and provides the
    screen information required for knockback boundary handling.

The player facing direction comes from the existing player
system.
